# How Annotshot compares

Tuesday 2026-11-03 · Annotshot 0.1 (the first version)

Annotshot is a first version with one job: capture part of the screen, mark it up with PowerPoint-style text boxes and shapes, then copy or save it. The tools below are mature and most do far more. This page is meant to help you choose, not to sell Annotshot.

Facts about other tools were checked against their own websites or documentation on the date above, with the links listed under Sources. "Unverified" means we could not confirm the fact from a reliable page. Prices are in US dollars and change often.

## Tool by tool

**Snagit.** The most complete tool here. It has scrolling capture, video recording, a library of past captures, sharing, and many callout styles. Its Callout tool does what Annotshot does: a text box with its own fill and its own font color. If you capture every day, record videos, or need to find last month's screenshots, Snagit is the better buy. Annotshot is simpler: no account, no subscription, and the editor shows one row of picture actions, one row of tools and one property strip.

**ShareX.** Free, open source and very powerful on Windows, with scrolling capture, recording and uploads to many services. Its editor has many tools that Annotshot lacks, such as blur, pixelate, step numbers and a magnifier. The plain Text tool has no background fill. The Speech Balloon does have one, but it comes with a pointer tail. ShareX does not save an editable project, so once the image is saved, the text is flat. Its settings are deep, and first-time users can find them overwhelming. Annotshot has fewer features and fewer settings.

**Greenshot.** Free, open source, light and quick on Windows. It saves an editable `.greenshot` file, as Annotshot does, and has obfuscation (hiding sensitive parts) and many export options. Its elements can have a fill color. An older user report says the text color is tied to the border color; we could not confirm whether that is still true. The Mac version in the App Store has not been updated since 2020. Annotshot's difference is that text color, fill and border are three separate controls, with no dependency between them.

**FastStone Capture.** A one-time purchase with a long feature list: scrolling capture, screen recording with a video editor, and a lossless `.fsc` format that keeps annotations editable. It is Windows only. We could not confirm whether its text boxes take a fill color separate from the text color. If you want recording and scrolling capture without a subscription, FastStone is the stronger choice. Annotshot is free and has a smaller editor.

**CleanShot X.** A polished Mac-only app with scrolling capture, recording, cloud hosting, capture history and editable project files. Its text tool has seven preset styles; we could not confirm that you can pick any fill color for a text box. On a Mac, CleanShot X is far more complete than Annotshot. Annotshot is free, runs on Windows and Linux too, and gives each text box a free choice of text color and fill.

**Flameshot.** Free and open source on Linux, Windows and macOS, and quick to use: select an area, draw on it, copy or save. It has no documented text background, so text can be hard to read on a busy screenshot (an open request in its issue tracker asks for better contrast). It does not save an editable project. Flameshot's capture is the more mature on Linux. Annotshot adds boxed text and the ability to reopen and re-edit your work.

**Windows Snipping Tool.** Already on every Windows 10 and 11 PC, with region, window, full-screen and freeform capture, video snips, a pen, a highlighter, shapes and text recognition. A text tool was reported in testing in late 2025, but we could not confirm a background fill or a general release. It has no scrolling capture and no editable save. For a quick snip with a pen mark, it is all most people need. Annotshot is for the moment you want a readable label in a box.

## Who Annotshot is for

Annotshot is for someone who wants to put PowerPoint-style text boxes on a screenshot in three clicks, and nothing else: capture, click **Text** and type, pick a **Fill**, then **Copy image** and paste it into a slide, a Teams message or an email. It keeps an editable copy if you ask for one. It does not record video, scroll long pages, organize a library or upload anywhere. If you need any of those, one of the tools above is the better choice. Annotshot is a first version, so expect rough edges, and expect the list of missing features to stay short on purpose.
